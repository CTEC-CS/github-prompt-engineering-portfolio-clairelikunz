# About Me

## Who I Am
  My name is Claire Kunz, I am a current senior at Alta High School, and class of 2030 at the University of Utah. A multitude of things interest me about AI but most notably is how AI implication will change the work force and daily life as we know it. 
  As a student who aspires to work in Computer Science and Cybersecurity, I know that to be in the tech field you must be willing to constantly evolve your knowledge. Without the drive to learn more, one risks being left behind as the world experiences changes. 
  I'm excited to see how AI will be implemented into cyber fields and what changes I will get to experience as I watch the work force evolve.

  I want to focus on the Computer Science and Cybersecurity fields because those are the two fields I am most interested in working in the future. I also would like to focus on student privacy and safety when using AI so that students can have access to more AI tools while learning how to use it responsibly. 
  Computer Science and Cybersecurity fields matter to me because I grew up playing video games, going to cybersecurity conferences, and even developing my own websites for fun! That being said, I am a mental health advocate and plan to implement an informative LLM into my mental health resource website so that students can have access to help without the fear of judgement.
  I hope to one day extend reliable AI resources to students in my community so by researching AI privacy, I can implement safety filters so that students information can be protected.

## What Drives Me
    I want to utilize AI to make mental health resources more accessible for students while emphasizing student privacy. As a student who struggles with mental health, I feel having more judgement-free and readily accessible resources could've helped me immensely. I am exploring an intersection between AI, mental health, and user privacy in order to ensure students can access resources in an ethical manner. I believe AI should be used to assist and leverage human capabilities. Specifically, AI should be used to complete time-consuming tasks quicker and more efficiently so that more time can be put into elaborate and complex tasks.

## About This Portfolio
    This portfolio outlines my work in prompt engineering starting in 2026. Provided will be prompts I have engineered, the accompanied explanations, and real world scenario demonstartions for each prompt. I built this portfolio to track my progress in prompt engineering and organize my work in a way that is easily accessable. 

Portfolio Content:
- Professionally crafted, tested prompts
- Complete documentation of design decisions
- Real examples demonstrating each prompt

My Approach:
    When prompt engineering, I focus on giving the AI clear and decisive guidance first. After giving clear instructions with background information, a role, and a task, I create a section titled for the AI. In this section, I provide guidance on how the response should be formatted as well as a template for the expected response.
